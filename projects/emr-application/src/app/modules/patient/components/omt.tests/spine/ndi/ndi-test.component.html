Neck Disability Index Questionnaire

[Instructions]

This questionnaire is designed to enable us to understand how much your neck pain has affected your ability to manage everyday activities. Please answer each Section by selecting the ONE CHOICE that most applies to you.

We realize that you may feel that more than one statement may relate to you, but Please just circle the one choice which closely describes your problem right now.

Scoring

Simply count up the points and plug the total in below: For each question there is a possible of 5 points: 0 for the first question, 1 for the second question, 2 for the third question etc.

Total score = SUM (points for all 10 sections)

Disability in percent = (total score) / 50 * 100

If not all of the questions are answered then disability in percent = (total score) / (5 * (number of questions answered)) * 100

Interpretation:

0 - 4 = no disability

5 - 14 = mild disability

15 - 24 = moderate disability

25 - 34 = severe disability

above 34 = complete disability
