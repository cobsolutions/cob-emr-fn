Foot and Ankle Ability Measure - Sports Subscale

[Instructions]

For the most valid results it is suggested for the FAAM Sports subscale be generated only when subjects completed 90% or more of the items (7 of 8)

Sports Subscale:

The response to each item on the Sports subscale is scored from 4 to 0, with 4 being 'no difficulty' and 0 being 'unable to do'. N/A responses are not included in the calculation.

The score on each of the items are added together to get the item score total. The number of items with a response is multiplied by 4 to get the highest potential score.

Interpretation:

G-Code Modifiers are assigned by the Composite Score as follows:

  • 100% = CH (no difficulty)
  • 80 - 99% = CI
  • 60 - 79% = CJ
  • 40 - 59% = CK
  • 20 - 39% = CL
  • 1 - 19% = CM
  • 0% = CN (unable to do)
