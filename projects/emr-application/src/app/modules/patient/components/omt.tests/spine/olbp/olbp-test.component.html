Instructions

This questionnaire is designed to enable us to understand how much your low back pain has affected your ability to manage everyday activities.

  • Please answer each section by selecting the ONE CHOICE that most applies to you
  • Select the choice which closely describes your problem right now

Scoring: Total score = SUM (points for all 10 sections). Disability in percent = (total score) / 50 * 100

Interpretation:

  • 0% - 20% = Minimal disability
  • 21% - 40% = Moderate disability
  • 41% - 60% = Severe disability
  • 61% - 80% = Crippled
  • 81% - 100% = Bed-bound or exaggerating symptoms
