Tinetti Balance and Gait Assessment

[Instructions]
Description:

The Tinetti assessment tool is an easily administered task-oriented test that measures an older adult's gait and balance abilities.

Equipment needed:
  • Hard armless chair
  • Stopwatch or wristwatch
  • 15 foot walkway
Completion Time:

10-15 minutes

Score Meaning:
  • <19 - High fall risk
  • 19-24 - Medium fall risk
  • 25-28 - Low fall risk
Balance Tests: Make sure patient is seated in a hard, armless chair.
