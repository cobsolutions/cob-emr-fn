Berg Balance Scale

Instructions

Description: 14-item scale designed to measure balance of the older adult in a clinical setting.

Equipment needed:

  • Ruler
  • 2 standard chairs (one with arm rests, one without)
  • Footstool or step
  • Stopwatch or wristwatch
  • 15 foot walkway

Completion Time: 15-20 minutes

General Instructions: Please document each task and/or give instructions as written. When scoring, please record the lowest response category that applies for each item.

Score Interpretation:

  • 0-20 = High fall risk
  • 21-40 = Medium fall risk
  • 41-56 = Low fall risk
