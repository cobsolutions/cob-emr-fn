Activities-specific Balance Confidence (ABC) Scale

Instructions

For each of the following, please indicate your level of confidence in doing the activity without losing your balance or becoming unsteady from choosing one of the percentage points on the scale from 0% to 100%.

  • If you do not currently do the activity in question, try and imagine how confident you would be if you had to do the activity
  • If you normally use a walking aid to do the activity or hold onto someone, rate your confidence as if you were using these supports

Scoring: Total the ratings (possible range = 0 - 1600) and divide by 16 to get each subject's ABC score.

Interpretation:

  • ≥80% = High level of physical functioning
  • 50-80% = Moderate level of physical functioning
  • <50% = Low level of physical functioning
  • <67% = Older adults at risk for falling
