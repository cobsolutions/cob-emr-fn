Account Deactivated

Your account has been deactivated by an administrator. You no longer have access to the system.

Access Restricted You are unable to log in or use any system features while your account is inactive. All your data, records, and history remain safely stored and will be available if your account is reactivated.

What you can do

1
Contact your administrator Reach out to your organization's system administrator to inquire about the reason your account was deactivated.
2
Request reactivation Ask your administrator to reactivate your account from the User Control Panel if appropriate.
3
Log back in Once your account has been reactivated, you can log in again and resume using the system normally.
Your data is safe. All patient records, clinical notes, and account history are preserved and will not be deleted during the deactivation period.
